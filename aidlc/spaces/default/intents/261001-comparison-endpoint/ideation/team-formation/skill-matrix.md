- Use occasional engineering reviews to check the integration boundary and technical decisions; the reviewer and review points are not yet named.
- Reassess security and operations skills after hosting and exposure decisions are made.
- No external partner or contractor is planned. If an internal gap remains, record it and seek a separate decision before changing the staffing plan.
